Fasted Cardio

3–4×/week
20–40min
LOWintensity

Do it first thing in the morning before eating — incline walk, easy bike, or stair-stepper. Keep it conversational pace (you could still talk). On non-lifting days is ideal so it doesn't sap your strength sessions.

  • Keep it easy & under 60 min. Low intensity burns a higher share of fat and won't eat into recovery. Save anything high-intensity for fed states.
  • Protein is your muscle insurance. Hit at least ~0.7–1 g per lb bodyweight daily (~120–170g for you). This is what actually protects muscle in a fast far more than cardio timing.
  • Optional safeguard: if you feel weak or are worried about muscle, 10–20g protein (a scoop of whey or EAAs) before cardio blunts breakdown with minimal effect on fat burning.
  • Eat after. Have a protein + carb meal within an hour of finishing.

Reality check: research shows fasted vs. fed cardio gives the same fat loss long-term when calories match — the deficit and consistency are what drive results. Fasted cardio is a convenient tool, not magic. Pick whatever you'll actually stick to.
